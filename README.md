Introduction to Movement Ecology with R
================

This course provides an introduction to advanced methods to analyze
animal movement data using R. Participants will learn how to fit
habitat-selection functions, step-selection functions and hidden Markov
models.

# Schedule

## Day 1

1.  Introduction and recap (J)
2.  HSF/RSFs (B)
3.  SSFs 1 (J)

## Day 2

4.  SSF 2 (B)

# Recommended reading

- Avgar, T., Potts, J. R., Lewis, M. A., & Boyce, M. S. (2016).
  Integrated step selection analysis: bridging the gap between resource
  selection and animal movement. Methods in Ecology and Evolution, 7(5),
  619-630.

- Fortin, D., Beyer, H. L., Boyce, M. S., Smith, D. W., Duchesne, T., &
  Mao, J. S. (2005). Wolves influence elk movements: behavior shapes a
  trophic cascade in Yellowstone National Park. Ecology, 86(5),
  1320-1330.

- Michelot, T., Klappstein, N. J., Potts, J. R., & Fieberg, J. (2023).
  Understanding step selection analysis through numerical integration.

- Forester, J. D., Im, H. K., & Rathouz, P. J. (2009). Accounting for
  animal movement in estimation of resource selection functions:
  sampling and data analysis. Ecology, 90(12), 3554-3565.

  P., Leclerc, M., … & Gerber, B. D. (2022). Conceptual and
  methodological advances in habitat‐selection modeling: guidelines for

  selection analyses. Ecology and evolution, 9(2), 880-890.

- Thurfjell, H., Ciuti, S., & Boyce, M. S. (2014). Applications of
  step-selection functions in ecology and conservation. Movement
  ecology, 2(1), 1-12.
